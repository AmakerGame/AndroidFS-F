AndroidFS-F is installed.

Start AndroidFS-F from the Start menu. In the window, plug in an Android phone
with USB debugging enabled and allow this computer on the phone screen. The phone
appears in the device list and is mounted as the first free drive letter,
starting at Q:.

Supported phones: arm64-v8a and armeabi-v7a.

Settings and the log are stored in %APPDATA%\AndroidFS-F. adb.exe must be on
PATH, or set its location in Settings.

A restart may be required for the Dokan driver to load.
